Canyon Crossing

Build a bridge between the two cliffs. Keep the cost under budget, then drive the delivery truck safely to the other side.

HOW TO PLAY
1. Pick a material.
2. Tap one glowing point, then another to connect them.
3. Build a road deck across the gap and support it with beams or cables.
4. Press TEST DRIVE and see if your bridge holds!

Wood is cheap, steel is strong, and cables are great for hanging support. Try to stay under budget to earn stars.
